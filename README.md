# FUTURE_SELF

A retro-terminal, self-contained web app for subconscious reprogramming.

## What It Is

You define your future self — not as goals, but as present-tense facts — then read them every morning and night until they become your default operating system.

The app tracks your daily reading streak, enforces the morning/night ritual, and stores everything locally in your browser.

## The Vibe

ASCII-minimal: monospace typography, `+` corner accents, `>` prompts, zero gradients, no rounded corners, no icons. Pure black and white. Inverts for night mode.

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in any browser.
2. Fill out 5 identity axioms + 5 emotional states (write them as if you are already that person).
3. Lock in your identity.
4. Read it every morning before input, every night before sleep.
5. Check off each session to build your streak.

## Data

Everything lives in `localStorage`. No server, no accounts, no tracking. Export your data anytime from the Config tab.
